EOW Odisha attaches properties in Surya Nirman multi-investor fraud case

Bhubaneswar, 15 Oct 2023

The Economic Offences Wing (EOW), CID-CB, Bhubaneswar has secured ad-interim attachment of landed properties worth Rs 2.07 crore belonging to M/s Surya Nirman Resources Pvt Ltd and its directors in connection with EOW P.S. Case No. 07/2023 dated 21.02.2023 under Sections 419/420/467/468/471 IPC and Section 6 of the Odisha Protection of Interests of Depositors (OPID) Act.

Attached Properties

The following properties situated at Village Mendhasala, Tahasil Bhubaneswar, District Khordha have been attached:

  1. Plot No. 415 of Khata No. 94, total area 1.075 acres, in the name of Krushnachandra Barajena, S/o Bauribandhu Barajena, resident of Nijagaon, Khordha.
  2. Plot No. 309, Khata No. 17, measuring 0.50 acres, Mouza Mendhasala, in the name of Purti Bibhag (Government).
  3. Plot No. 128, Khata No. 17, measuring 0.42 acres, in the name of Suryamani Sahoo, S/o Arjuna Sahoo, Village Mendhasala.

Persons Arrested

The following persons have been arrested in connection with the above case:

  1. Arun Kumar Sahu, Director, M/s Surya Nirman Resources Pvt Ltd, aged 48 years, resident of Patia, Bhubaneswar.
  2. Pradeep Kumar Mohanty, Director, M/s Surya Nirman Resources Pvt Ltd, aged 51 years, resident of Chandrasekharpur, Bhubaneswar.
  3. Sangita Devi, Promoter, M/s Surya Nirman Resources Pvt Ltd, aged 42 years, resident of Saheed Nagar, Bhubaneswar.

Public Notice

Any person having any claim or objection to the said attached properties may file the same before the undersigned within 30 days from the date of publication of this notice.

Issued by: Office of the Deputy Superintendent of Police, EOW, CID-CB, Bhubaneswar.

Date of attachment: 12 October 2023.
