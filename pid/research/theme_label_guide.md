# PID Theme Label Guide

> Use this guide when reviewing Phase 0 seed examples. A label means the artifact
> contains evidence for a theme. It does not mean the property, seller, or location is
> conclusively defective.

---

## Review Principles

1. Label source facts, not suspicions.
2. Separate allegation from proved fact.
3. Separate source fact from reviewer inference.
4. Do not use a place name as the risk reason.
5. Do not approve a theme label from a negative search alone.
6. Require order/deed/record text for property-specific labels when party-name search can over-match.
7. Keep "what this source cannot prove" explicit.

---

## Evidence Levels

| Level | Meaning | Use |
|---|---|---|
| E0 | Raw artifact captured | Store only; no theme promotion |
| E1 | Parsed fact | Can support candidate label |
| E2 | Linked evidence | Can support pending-review label |
| E3 | Human reviewed | Can support approved seed example |
| E4 | Pattern evidence | Counts toward pattern promotion |
| E5 | Insight support | Counts toward aggregate insight/rate |

Phase 0 examples should aim for E3. Automated-only examples remain candidates.

## Title and Ownership Labels

### A-01 Undisclosed Co-Owner / Partial Title Sale

Approve when one or more source facts show:

- RoR lists multiple owners or share fractions.
- Seller/claimant appears to represent sole authority despite co-owner rows.
- Court/revenue case names co-owners or family members in a title/partition dispute.
- Registered deed or EC shows fractional share transfer, but later claim treats land as whole.

Do not approve from:

- multiple plot rows under one khata without owner-row proof
- surname similarity alone
- unreviewed fixture data without raw artifact

### A-02 Active Partition / Lis Pendens

Approve when source facts show:

- active/pending partition suit
- civil suit involving inherited/family property
- recent hearing/order in a partition/title matter
- order text mentions disputed sale, partition, possession, or alienation

Do not approve from:

- old disposed case without reading outcome
- party-name hit with no property/order linkage

### A-03 Succession / Unrecorded Heirs

Approve when source facts show:

- seller differs from RoR owner and no mutation chain is present
- order/case discusses legal heirs, intestacy, probate, succession certificate, or inherited title
- RoR owner appears ancestral/deceased and transaction depends on heirship

Do not approve from:

- name mismatch alone without context
- buyer claim that owner is deceased unless supported by artifact or reviewer note

### A-04 Seller Impersonation / Identity Fraud

Approve when source facts show:

- criminal/civil case alleging impersonation, forged ID, forged signature, fake owner, or false PoA
- deed/court artifact records different identity markers for same supposed owner
- PoA/authority challenged as forged or invalid

Do not approve from:

- transliteration variance alone
- NRI/absentee owner status alone

### A-05 Multiple Sale / Double Registration

Approve when source facts show:

- competing sale deeds for same property
- cancellation suit or injunction based on earlier sale
- EC/deed timeline shows repeated sale of same plot/khata by same seller without clear chain
- mutation lag creates conflicting purchaser claims

Do not approve from:

- multiple normal transactions over a long chain without conflict
- duplicate search result rows

### A-06 PoA Authority Defect

Approve when source facts show:

- sale was executed by PoA and authority is disputed
- PoA is alleged forged, revoked, expired, unregistered, or used after principal death
- court/deed artifact identifies PoA defect as central issue

Do not approve from:

- any PoA sale by itself; the defect must be evidenced

## Government Land and Classification Labels

### B-01 Anabadi / Government Waste Land Sale

Approve when source facts show:

- Kisam/class/status contains Anabadi, Sarkari, government, jungle, or similar government/waste classification
- court/revenue case involves government land settlement, encroachment, or private sale challenge
- missing settlement/conversion proof is identified by source or reviewer

Do not approve from:

- unknown kisam
- generic agricultural classification

### B-02 Forest / Eco-Sensitive Misrepresentation

Approve when source facts show:

- official forest/protected area/eco-sensitive overlay intersects or buffers the property
- order/notice mentions forest land, reserved forest, protected forest, sanctuary, or clearance
- credible reviewed artifact connects plot/project to forest restriction

Do not approve from:

- tree canopy alone
- "green-looking" satellite image without official/legal layer

### B-03 Ceiling / Bhoodan / Vesting Issue

Approve when source facts show:

- ceiling, vesting, Bhoodan, OLR, OEA, or settlement dispute in RoR remarks, revenue case, or order
- large agricultural holdings with explicit ceiling/vesting record references

Do not approve from:

- acreage alone
- old settlement date alone

### B-04 Conversion / Land Use Defect

Approve when source facts show:

- revenue class incompatible with intended use and no conversion artifact is present
- conversion refusal/pending case/order
- official planning or revenue record requires conversion for proposed use

Do not approve from:

- Bhulekh kisam alone unless intended use is documented

## Encumbrance and Financial Labels

### C-01 CERSAI Active Charge

Approve when source facts show:

- CERSAI active/open charge
- unsatisfied charge
- secured creditor and borrower/chargor fields
- property description reasonably linked to the target property or seller corpus event

Do not approve from:

- no-result search
- satisfied charge unless the theme is historical financial context
- same-name borrower without property/entity linkage

### C-02 Registered Mortgage / EC Encumbrance

Approve when source facts show:

- EC/deed artifact lists mortgage, charge, lien, lease, attachment, or court order affecting property
- mortgage deed remains unresolved in timeline

Do not approve from:

- EC instructions alone
- user statement of loan without artifact

### C-03 Private / Informal Lender Dispute

Approve when source facts show:

- court case alleges private loan, hand loan, possessory security, unregistered mortgage, agreement to sell as security, or money-lender possession
- CERSAI/EC clean state exists but litigation indicates private encumbrance

Do not approve from:

- informal rumor without reviewed artifact

### C-04 DRT / SARFAESI Distress

Approve when source facts show:

- DRT/DRAT case against seller/entity
- SARFAESI proceeding, recovery certificate, bank possession notice, or High Court SARFAESI challenge
- lender party and borrower/entity match

Do not approve from:

- same-name party hit without borrower/entity confirmation

### C-05 PMLA / ED / Attachment

Approve when source facts show:

- ED/PMLA/attachment order involving property or seller/entity
- court/official release references attachment or proceeds of crime

Do not approve from:

- general criminal case without attachment/property relevance

## Physical and Possession Labels

### D-01 Boundary Encroachment

Approve when source facts show:

- survey/boundary dispute case
- Bhunaksha/WFS geometry materially mismatches RoR/deed/seller area after unit reconciliation
- neighbor dispute references boundary, demarcation, encroachment, or survey

Do not approve from:

- small area differences within tolerance
- map artifact alone without mismatch or dispute

### D-02 Possession Conflict

Approve when source facts show:

- injunction/possession suit
- third-party occupant or dispossession allegation in order
- police/civil complaint about possession linked to property

Do not approve from:

- owner absent from property without dispute evidence

### D-03 Access / Right-of-Way Defect

Approve when source facts show:

- easement/pathway/right-of-way dispute
- map indicates no road access and reviewer confirms practical access issue
- deed/court record mentions access obstruction

Do not approve from:

- OSM missing road alone

### D-04 Subdivision / Layout Anomaly

Approve when source facts show:

- many small carved plots from agricultural parent land plus missing layout approval evidence
- project/layout sold without RERA/development authority support
- common road/open-space ambiguity in artifacts

Do not approve from:

- small plot size alone

## Regulatory Labels

### E-01 Zoning / Land Use Mismatch

Approve when source facts show:

- official master plan/development authority zone conflicts with proposed use
- conversion/approval required and missing
- project marketing conflicts with official permitted use

Do not approve from:

- RoR kisam alone

### E-02 Acquisition / LARR Corridor

Approve when source facts show:

- SIA, LARR, Gazette, collector notice, or acquisition litigation
- village/project/plot reference links to affected area
- sale timing overlaps with notification window

Do not approve from:

- infrastructure project nearby without notification/official layer

### E-03 Unauthorized Project / Layout

Approve when source facts show:

- project/layout appears to require RERA/development approval and no registration/approval artifact is found after documented search
- RERA cancelled/expired/suspended status
- authority order against layout/promoter

Do not approve from:

- individual resale transaction

### E-04 Environmental / Flood Restriction

Approve when source facts show:

- official flood/forest/wetland/eco-sensitive/CRZ buffer applies
- official restriction layer or notice is linked to property/project

Do not approve from:

- terrain or visual impression alone

## Actor and Network Labels

### F-01 Aggregator / Land Flipper

Approve when source facts show:

- repeat purchases/sales by same entity
- short holding periods
- repeated counterparties across deeds
- multi-property or multi-district portfolio pattern

Do not approve from:

- one resale transaction

### F-02 Repeat Litigant Seller

Approve when source facts show:

- multiple property/title/financial cases involving same seller/entity
- repeated similar causes of action

Do not approve from:

- unrelated common-name hits

### F-03 Promoter Distress Network

Approve when source facts show:

- promoter/company has RERA complaints, DRT/High Court recovery, insolvency, or charge records
- network links between promoter, directors, projects, and distressed cases

Do not approve from:

- one complaint without entity confirmation

### F-04 Name-Variant Entity Risk

Approve when source facts show:

- same actor appears under variants across deeds/courts/charges after strong anchors
- PAN/company ID/document ID links variants

Do not approve from:

- fuzzy name match alone
